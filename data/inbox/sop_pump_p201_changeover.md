# SOP-U200-07 Rev 2: Lean Solvent Pump Changeover P-201A / P-201B

**Unit:** 200 (Vapour Processing)
**Applies to:** P-201A, P-201B (horizontal centrifugal, packed gland)
**Revision note:** Rev 2 adds the 60-second dead-head limit after the
P-201B motor trip investigation (WO-3039).

## 1. Before changeover

1. Confirm standby pump suction valve fully open and casing flooded -
   crack the vent until solvent appears.
2. Verify packing gland drips at the running pump are normal (20-40
   drips/minute). Zero drips overheats packing; a stream means repack
   (see WO-3011).
3. Standby motor space heater OFF at least 30 minutes before start.

## 2. Changeover sequence

1. Start standby against a cracked-open discharge valve.
2. Open standby discharge fully within 60 seconds of start. Running
   dead-headed longer trips the motor on overload - this is what took
   P-201B out in November (WO-3039).
3. Once discharge pressures equalise, close the outgoing pump discharge,
   then its suction. Keep gland flush in service until the pump is cool.

## 3. After changeover

1. Log changeover time and pump hours in the shift log.
2. Take a vibration reading on the newly running pump within 24 hours -
   the BEARING-WEAR finding on P-201A (WO-3064) was caught this way.
3. Leave the outgoing pump flooded and valved-in as standby.
