# Incident Report IR-2026-030

**Date of incident:** 2026-03-19, 14:20 hrs
**Unit:** 200 (Fired Heater)
**Classification:** Condition finding — no loss of containment
**Related work order:** WO-2307

## What happened

Routine firebox inspection of fired heater F-201 found two radiant tubes
bulged near burner 3. Bulging indicates localized overheating, most likely from
flame impingement or restricted flow through those passes.

## Findings

- Bulge diameter growth consistent with short-term overheat, not creep.
- Burner 3 showed an uneven flame pattern; air register found partially seized.
- No through-wall defect; containment intact.

## Governing requirement

F-201 fired equipment is inspected under OISD-STD-105 (fire-fighting and fired
equipment safety practice). The bulged tubes are a fitness-for-service item:
they must be assessed against the retirement limit and either monitored on a
tight interval or replaced at the next turnaround.

## Actions

1. Firing on burner 3 derated pending register repair (done).
2. Bulged tubes flagged for replacement at turnaround (WO-2307).
3. Burner management review for flame pattern across the row.
