# Incident Report IR-2026-021

**Date of incident:** 2026-02-18, 03:40 hrs
**Unit:** 300 (Flare and Relief)
**Classification:** Near miss — premature relief, no release to atmosphere
**Related work order:** WO-2304

## What happened

During a minor pressure excursion on the reactor loop, relief valve PSV-301
lifted approximately 0.9 barg below its set pressure of 14.0 barg. The lift was
brief and reseated, but an early-lifting PSV on a reaction loop is a protection
concern: it can chatter and lose capacity when it is actually needed.

## Findings

Bench testing (WO-2304) found the simmer margin had collapsed — the valve was
weeping and lifting early. Root cause was traced to spring relaxation combined
with light polymer deposits on the seat from the reactor effluent.

## Governing requirement

PSV-301 is a statutory relief device inspected under IBR and OISD-STD-132. The
early lift means the last pop test result no longer represents the valve's
condition; the inspection date has effectively been invalidated and a fresh
test is required before the loop returns to full rate.

## Actions

1. PSV-301 reset and re-certified (done).
2. Review effluent composition for polymer-forming conditions.
3. Shorten the pop-test interval for PSV-301 to annual until two clean tests.
