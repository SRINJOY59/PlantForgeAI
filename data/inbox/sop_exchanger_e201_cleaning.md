# SOP-U200-09 — Heat Exchanger E-201 Bundle Cleaning

**Unit:** 200
**Equipment:** Feed/effluent exchanger E-201
**Governing standard:** API-570 (piping and in-service inspection interface)
**Revision:** 2 (2022-11)

## 1. When to clean

E-201 fouls on the shell side. Trigger a cleaning when shell-side differential
pressure rises more than 30 percent over clean baseline, or the approach
temperature climbs 8 C above design — whichever comes first. Both have driven
recent work orders (WO-2301, WO-2309).

## 2. Isolation

1. Reduce Unit 200 rate to minimum stable.
2. Isolate E-201 per the unit isolation plan; anything downstream of the feed
   pump P-201 that depends on the exchanger must be brought down first.
3. Drain, flush, and gas-free before opening.

## 3. Cleaning

1. Pull the tube bundle. Inspect tubes for wall loss — eddy-current findings on
   E-201 have shown localized 40 percent loss (INS-515); plug tubes past the
   retirement limit rather than returning them to service.
2. Hydroblast the shell side and tube outer surfaces.
3. Pressure-test before reinstalling.

## 4. Post-job

Log the cleaning against the exchanger and note the fouling rate. A shortening
interval between cleanings is the signal to review the antifoulant program.
