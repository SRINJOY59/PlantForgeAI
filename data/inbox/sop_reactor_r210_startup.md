# SOP-U200-14 — Reactor R-210 Startup and Bed Conditioning

**Unit:** 200 (Reaction Section)
**Equipment:** Fixed-bed reactor R-210, feed exchanger E-201, feed pump P-201
**Governing standard:** OISD-STD-116 (fired and reaction equipment operating practice)
**Revision:** 3 (2023-06)

## 1. Purpose

Bring R-210 online from a cold, depressured state, conditioning the catalyst
bed without exceeding the maximum bed temperature rise.

## 2. Prerequisites

- E-201 commissioned and passing feed at design approach temperature.
- P-201 lined up with suction strainer verified clean (see WO history).
- PSV-301 on the reactor loop verified in service and within its pop-test date
  per OISD-STD-132.
- Nitrogen purge complete; oxygen below 0.5 percent at the vent.

## 3. Procedure

1. Establish nitrogen circulation through R-210 at 30 percent design rate.
2. Begin controlled heat-up at no more than 25 C/hr, watching the bed
   differential pressure. A rising dP indicates fines migration — hold and
   investigate before proceeding (see WO-2303).
3. At 180 C bed temperature, introduce feed slowly via P-201, ramping over
   two hours.
4. Watch the reactor bed temperature rise across each thermocouple. If any
   segment rises more than 40 C above its neighbour, cut feed immediately —
   this is a runaway precursor.
5. Once stable, hand over to the panel for normal-rate operation.

## 4. Notes

Recurring high bed dP on R-210 has been linked to catalyst screen fouling.
Screen inspection at every turnaround is recommended until the trend stabilises.
