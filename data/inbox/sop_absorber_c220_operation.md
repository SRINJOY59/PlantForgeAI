# SOP-U200-03 Rev 1: Absorber Column C-220 - Normal Operation

**Unit:** 200 (Vapour Processing)
**Applies to:** C-220 absorber, V-210 KO drum, lean solvent pumps P-201A/B
**Reference:** OISD-STD-179, process datasheet PD-C220-02

## 1. Normal lineup

1. Vapour from K-301 discharge passes aftercooler E-210 into KO drum V-210.
2. V-210 boot level between 30% and 60% on LT-212. Liquid carryover into
   the column is the single most common upset - see IR-2025-058.
3. Lean solvent to the top tray from P-201A (P-201B standby).

## 2. Shift checks

1. Column differential pressure below 180 mbar at design rates. A rising
   DP trend with steady rates indicates foaming - dose antifoam per lab
   guidance and log it in the shift report.
2. LT-212 against field gauge weekly (transmitter failed frozen once,
   WO-3046 - the reading can look plausible while dead).
3. Solvent strength sample every Tuesday to the lab.

## 3. Rate changes

1. Change feed rates at no more than 10% per 15 minutes. Fast swings pull
   liquid off the V-210 demister - the pad collapse found in WO-3052
   followed repeated fast ramp-ups.
2. After any rate change, watch column DP for 30 minutes.

## 4. Pump changeover P-201A/B

Follow SOP-U200-07 for changeover. Do not start the standby against a
closed discharge valve for more than 60 seconds - the November motor trip
(WO-3039) was caused by an extended dead-head start.

## 5. Records

Log DP, LT-212 level, antifoam dosing and solvent strength in the shift
log. Reference this SOP in any work order raised on C-220 or V-210.
