# SOP-U100-07 Rev 3: Mechanical Seal Replacement — Centrifugal Pumps P-101A/B

**Unit:** 100 (Feed Section)
**Applies to:** P-101A, P-101B (centrifugal, single-stage, John Crane type 5610 cartridge seal)
**Supersedes:** SOP-U100-07 Rev 2 (2023)
**Reference:** OISD-STD-119 (inspection of pumps), OEM manual KSB-MM-4402

## 1. Safety prerequisites

1. Obtain cold work permit from shift in-charge.
2. Electrically isolate the pump at MCC and hang LOTO tag. Verify zero energy by attempting local start.
3. Close suction valve from T-101 and discharge valve to E-204. Drain casing through low-point drain to closed drain header.
4. If the pump handled hot feed within the last 4 hours, allow casing to cool below 60 C before opening.

## 2. Removal

1. Disconnect seal flush line from seal pot. Cap open ends immediately.
2. Remove coupling guard and spacer.
3. Back off gland bolts evenly, half a turn at a time, in a star pattern.
4. Withdraw seal cartridge using the OEM extraction tool only. Do not pry against the shaft sleeve.

## 3. Installation

1. Inspect shaft sleeve for scoring. Replace if wear exceeds 0.05 mm.
2. Fit new cartridge seal. Setting plates must remain in place until gland bolts are torqued.
3. Torque gland bolts to **45 Nm** in a star pattern (Rev 2 specified 40 Nm — superseded after seal face distortion findings in 2024).
4. Remove setting plates. Rotate shaft by hand: two full turns, no rubbing.
5. Reconnect seal flush line. Fill seal pot with clean flush fluid.

## 4. Pre-start checks

1. Confirm suction pressure at PI-102 reads at least **0.8 barg** before start. Repeated seal failures on P-101A have been traced to running below minimum suction pressure (cavitation).
2. Vent casing until liquid appears at vent.
3. Start against a cracked-open discharge valve; open fully once flow is confirmed on FT-103.
4. Observe seal faces for 15 minutes. Zero visible leakage is the acceptance criterion.

## 5. Records

Log seal batch number, torque values, and PI-102 reading at start in the work order before closing it.
