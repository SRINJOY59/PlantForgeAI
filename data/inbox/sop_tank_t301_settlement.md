# SOP-U300-11 — Storage Tank T-301 Settlement Survey

**Unit:** 300 (Storage)
**Equipment:** Atmospheric storage tank T-301
**Governing standard:** API-653 (aboveground storage tank inspection)
**Revision:** 5 (2023-09)

## 1. Purpose

Measure and evaluate the settlement of T-301's shell and bottom, so distortion
is caught before it threatens the shell-to-bottom weld or the floating roof
seal.

## 2. Survey

1. Survey the shell bottom at eight equally spaced points plus intermediate
   points on any suspect arc.
2. Record elevations against the last survey and the original baseline.
3. Evaluate differential settlement against API-653 Annex B — planar tilt is
   tolerable within limits; localized dishing near the shell is not.

## 3. Evaluation

The last survey (INS-512) found differential settlement within Annex B limits.
Continue on the current interval unless a step change appears. A sudden local
settlement near a shell course is a stop-and-investigate finding, not a
schedule item.

## 4. Records

Settlement data is part of the tank's API-653 file. The next external and
internal inspection dates are driven by the corrosion rate and the settlement
trend together, not by a fixed calendar.
