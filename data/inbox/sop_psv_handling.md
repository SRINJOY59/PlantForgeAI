# SOP-GEN-15 Rev 3: Pressure Safety Valve Removal, Testing and Reinstatement

**Units:** all (written after the PSV-204 lift event, IR-2026-014)
**Applies to:** all PSVs including PSV-204 (V-203, set 10.0 barg) and
PSV-210 (V-210, set 8.5 barg)
**Reference:** IBR, OISD-STD-132

## 1. Removal for testing

1. A PSV may only be removed when its protected equipment is depressured
   and isolated, OR a certified spare is installed in the same permit.
2. Record the as-found condition before cleaning: a valve suspected of
   passing in service (like PSV-210 in WO-3070) must be bench-checked
   as-found first - cleaning destroys the evidence.
3. Transport valves upright. A dropped or laid-down PSV fails its
   as-found test more often than not.

## 2. Bench testing

1. Pop test per OISD-STD-132; record set pressure, reseat pressure and
   seat tightness. PSV-204's post-lift recertification (WO-2251) is the
   reference example of a complete test record.
2. Any valve that lifted in service gets a full overhaul, not just a
   pop test.

## 3. Reinstatement

1. Verify the correct valve returns to the correct location - tag number
   stamped on the body must match the line list entry.
2. Torque inlet flange bolts to the piping class value; fit new gaskets
   both sides.
3. Remove the test gag. The permit is not closed until the gag removal
   is countersigned by the shift in-charge.
