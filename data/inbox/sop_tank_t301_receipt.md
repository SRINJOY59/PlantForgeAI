# SOP-U300-02 Rev 1: Product Receipt into Tank T-301

**Unit:** 300 (Liquid Handling)
**Applies to:** T-301 floating roof tank, transfer pump P-301, LT-304
**Reference:** OISD-STD-129, IR-2025-032 (learnings applied from the
T-101 level event)

## 1. Before receipt

1. Confirm available ullage from LT-304 AND a manual dip. The two must
   agree within 50 mm - LT-304 has a gauge-mismatch history (WO-4012),
   and the Unit 100 tank event (IR-2025-032) showed what a stuck
   transmitter can hide.
2. Walk the floating roof: no pooled water, drain valve open, seals
   seated. Pooled water after rain has blocked the drain before (WO-4019).
3. Line up receipt path; verify P-301 vented and primed - it loses prime
   after tank switches (WO-4005).

## 2. During receipt

1. Watch LT-304 trend continuously for the first 15 minutes, then each
   30 minutes. A frozen trend line is a failure sign, not a calm one.
2. Maximum fill rate 250 m3/h until the roof is fully floating, then
   400 m3/h.
3. Stop receipt at 85% and never exceed 90% - the margin exists for
   thermal expansion.

## 3. After receipt

1. Take a closing dip and reconcile with LT-304; log any difference
   above 50 mm and raise a work order if it repeats.
2. Close the receipt line and leave P-301 flooded.
