# OEM Calibration & Troubleshooting Manual: Fisher FIELDVUE DVC6200 Digital Valve Controller

### Calibration Procedure for TV-102 / HV-901
1. Connect Field Communicator to HART terminals on [TV-102](../04-final-control-elements/xmv-10-reactor-cooling-water-valve.md).
2. Execute Auto-Travel Calibration sequence.
3. Inspect magnetic feedback linkage for physical alignment to eliminate hysteresis ([IDV-14](../07-disturbance-library/idv-14.md)).
