# E-201 — Product Condenser (Master Data Sheet)

### 1. Equipment Summary & Specifications
Product Condenser E-201 is a horizontal shell-and-tube heat exchanger that receives hot off-gas (Stream 7) from Reactor R-101 and partially condenses unreacted monomers and heavy product fractions prior to entering Flash Separator D-301.

- **Manufacturer:** Thermal Exchanger Systems Corp. (Serial No. TE-4491)
- **TEMA Type:** BEM (Fixed Tubesheet, Single Pass Shell, Two Pass Tube)
- **Heat Transfer Duty:** 14.0 MW (47.8 MM BTU/hr)
- **Total Surface Area:** 480 m² (5,166 ft²)
- **Tube Side (Process Gas):** 480 tubes, 316L SS, 3/4" OD, 16 BWG. Operating pressure: 2.7 MPag. Design pressure: 4.5 MPag.
- **Shell Side (Cooling Water):** Carbon steel shell, site cooling water service. Controlled by valve [TV-201 (XMV-11)](../04-final-control-elements/xmv-11-condenser-cooling-water-valve.md). Operating pressure: 0.4 MPag. Design pressure: 0.9 MPag.

### 2. Instrumentation & Monitoring
- **Process Outlet Temp:** Monitored via [TT-301 (XMEAS-11)](../03-instrumentation/xmeas-11-separator-temperature.md) (Normal: 82.5 °C).
- **Cooling Water Outlet Temp:** [TT-201 (XMEAS-22)](../03-instrumentation/xmeas-22-condenser-cooling-water-temp.md) (Normal: 42.0 °C, Alarm High: 55.0 °C).

### 3. Inspection & Compliance Status
- **Eddy Current Tube Testing:** Last performed 2023-06-10 (**STATUS: OVERDUE** for 3-year mechanical integrity interval).
- **Known Operational Issue:** Shell-side biological fouling during summer ambient peaks ([IDV-05](../07-disturbance-library/idv-05.md)).
