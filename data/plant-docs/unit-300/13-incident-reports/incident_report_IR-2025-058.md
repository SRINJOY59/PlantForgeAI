# Incident Report IR-2025-058: K-401 Recycle Compressor High Vibration Trip

### Event Summary
On 2025-09-18, [K-401 Recycle Compressor](../02-equipment/k-401-recycle-compressor.md) tripped on radial bearing vibration (52 µm pk-pk) caused by liquid entrainment in overhead Stream 5 due to high level in [D-301 Separator](../02-equipment/d-301-separator.md).
