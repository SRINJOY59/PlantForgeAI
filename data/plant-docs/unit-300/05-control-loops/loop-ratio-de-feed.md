# FFC-102/103 — D:E Feed Ratio Controller (Grade/Selectivity Control)

- **PVs:** [FT-102 (XMEAS-02)](../03-instrumentation/xmeas-02-d-feed-flow.md), [FT-103 (XMEAS-03)](../03-instrumentation/xmeas-03-e-feed-flow.md)
- **Function:** Ratio control station — E feed setpoint is computed as a ratio of D feed flow; primary product-grade selectivity handle.
- **Typical ratio range:** 0.85–1.35 (E:D mass ratio) depending on target grade.
