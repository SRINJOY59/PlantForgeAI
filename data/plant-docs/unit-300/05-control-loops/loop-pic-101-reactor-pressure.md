# PIC-101 — Reactor Pressure Control

- **PV:** [PT-101 (XMEAS-07)](../03-instrumentation/xmeas-07-reactor-pressure.md)
- **OP:** Purge valve [HV-901 (XMV-06)](../04-final-control-elements/xmv-06-purge-valve.md) as primary; A-feed valve [FV-101 (XMV-03)](../04-final-control-elements/xmv-03-a-feed-valve.md) as secondary trim.
- **Setpoint:** 2.80 MPag
- **Notes:** Pressure control and inert (B) purge control are coupled — the purge valve is shared between pressure control and inert-buildup control.
