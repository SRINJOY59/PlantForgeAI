# SIC-401 — Compressor Anti-Surge Control

- **PV:** Computed surge margin from suction/discharge pressure and flow.
- **OP:** [HV-401 (XMV-05)](../04-final-control-elements/xmv-05-compressor-recycle-valve.md) recycle/kick-back valve.
- **Priority:** Override authority over normal recycle-flow optimization — surge protection always wins.
