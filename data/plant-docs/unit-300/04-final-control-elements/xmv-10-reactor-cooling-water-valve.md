# XMV-10 (TV-102) — Reactor Cooling Water Control Valve

- **Tag:** TV-102
- **Description:** Reactor Jacket & Coil Cooling Water Control Valve
- **Actuator Type:** Pneumatic spring-diaphragm with smart digital positioner.
- **Fail Position:** Fail Open (FO) — Loss of instrument air fully opens valve to prevent reactor overheating.
- **Normal Position:** 38% Open.
- **Maintenance Status:** **OVERDUE** for PM valve stroke test and positioner overhaul.
- **Associated Incident:** Mechanical binding of positioner feedback arm caused [IDV-14 / IR-2026-014](../07-disturbance-library/idv-14.md) thermal excursion.
