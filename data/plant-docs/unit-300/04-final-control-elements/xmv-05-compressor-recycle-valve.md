# XMV-05 (HV-401) — Compressor Anti-Surge Recycle Valve

- **Tag:** HV-401 (DCS Output: XMV-05)
- **Description:** 8-inch High-Speed Anti-Surge Control Valve on Compressor K-401 kick-back loop.
- **Fail Position:** Fail Open (FO) — Protects compressor from catastrophic aerodynamic surge on loss of power/air.
- **Normal Position:** 22% Open. Controlled by anti-surge loop [SIC-401](../05-control-loops/loop-sic-401-antisurge.md).
