# Unit 300 Process Hazard Analysis (HAZOP) Revalidation Report

### Executive Summary
Process Hazard Analysis (HAZOP) revalidation completed on 2023-11-14 per OSHA PSM 5-year mandate. Evaluated nodes: Node 1 (Reactor R-101 & Feeds), Node 2 (Condenser E-201 & Separator D-301), Node 3 (Recycle Compressor K-401 & Purge), Node 4 (Stripper T-501).

### Key Recommendations & Safety Action Items
1. **Node 1 Deviation — High Temperature in R-101:** Recommended adding secondary coil cooling interlock and upgrading [TV-102](../04-final-control-elements/xmv-10-reactor-cooling-water-valve.md) positioner to dual-acting SMART DVC6200.
2. **Node 3 Deviation — Low Recycle Flow in K-401:** Mandated fast-acting solenoid override on anti-surge valve [HV-401](../04-final-control-elements/xmv-05-compressor-recycle-valve.md) to prevent surge damage.
3. **Node 4 Deviation — Overpressure in T-501:** Verified sizing of [PSV-501](../06-safety-instrumented-system/psv-501.md) against blocked-outlet scenario.
