# OEM Operation & Maintenance Manual: R-101 Agitator Mechanical Seal

### Technical Specifications
- **Model:** Burgmann Cartex-M32 Double Cartridge Seal
- **Barrier Fluid:** Synthetic Mineral Oil ISO VG 32
- **Barrier Pressure:** 0.40 MPag (must be maintained 0.15 MPag ABOVE reactor operating pressure [PT-101](../03-instrumentation/xmeas-07-reactor-pressure.md)).

### Maintenance Instructions
1. Inspect barrier fluid reservoir level weekly.
2. Replace primary silicon carbide seal faces every 24,000 operating hours.
