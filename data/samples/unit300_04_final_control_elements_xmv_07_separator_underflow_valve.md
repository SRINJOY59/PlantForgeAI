# XMV-07 (LV-301) — Separator Liquid Underflow Control Valve

- **Tag:** LV-301 (DCS Output: XMV-07)
- **Description:** 4-inch Jacketed Control Valve regulating liquid resin flow (Stream 10) from Separator D-301 to Stripper T-501.
- **Fail Position:** Fail Closed (FC). Normal position: 48% Open. Controlled by level loop [LIC-301](../05-control-loops/loop-lic-301-separator-level.md).
