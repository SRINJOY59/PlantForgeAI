# Management of Change (MOC-2026-012): Catalyst Carrier Solvent Modification

### Proposal Description
Transition Ziegler-Natta catalyst slurry carrier solvent from Mineral Oil to Isododecane to improve catalyst dispersion efficiency in [R-101 Reactor](../02-equipment/r-101-reactor.md) and reduce byproduct F formation.
