# D-601 — Product Surge Drum

Horizontal insulated and electrical heat-traced surge drum (18.0 m³) that buffers stripped molten resin product downstream of Stripper T-501 prior to pumping by [P-601A/B](p-601a-product-pump.md) to Unit 310 Finishing for strand pelletization.
