# P-601A — Main Product Transfer Pump A

Positive displacement heavy-duty jacketed gear pump designed for molten high-viscosity polymer service (13.0 kg/s capacity, 45 kW motor). 100% installed spare paired with [P-601B](p-601b-product-pump.md) featuring automatic pressure-switch changeover.
