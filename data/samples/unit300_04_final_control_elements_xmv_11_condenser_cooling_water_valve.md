# XMV-11 (TV-201) — Condenser Cooling Water Control Valve

- **Tag:** TV-201 (DCS Output: XMV-11)
- **Description:** 6-inch Cooling Water Control Valve regulating flow to Condenser E-201 shell side.
- **Fail Position:** Fail Open (FO). Normal position: 35% Open. Monitored by [TT-201](../03-instrumentation/xmeas-22-condenser-cooling-water-temp.md).
