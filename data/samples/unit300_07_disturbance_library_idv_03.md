# IDV-03 — D Feed Temperature Step Change

**Description:** Simulates a vaporizer/heater upset on the 1-Butene feed skid.

### Impact & Fault Diagnosis
This disturbance case is part of the Unit 300 digital-twin fault-injection library. It is used to evaluate DCS control loop resilience, alarm generation, and AI-driven Root Cause Analysis (RCA) detection.
