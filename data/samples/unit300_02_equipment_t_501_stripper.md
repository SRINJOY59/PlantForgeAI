# T-501 — Polymer Resin Stripper Column

### 1. Equipment Specifications
Stripper T-501 is a 12-tray sieve tray distillation column that receives crude liquid resin from Separator D-301 (Stream 10). Live low-pressure steam (Stream 13) is injected at Tray 1 to strip dissolved unreacted monomers (1-Butene, VAM, Ethylene) out of the molten polymer resin.

- **Design Volume:** 42.0 m³ | 12 Stainless Steel Sieve Trays
- **Design Pressure / Temp:** 2.20 MPag / 180.0 °C
- **Normal Operating Pressure:** 1.08 MPag ([PT-501](../03-instrumentation/xmeas-16-stripper-pressure.md))
- **Normal Bottoms Temperature:** 65.0 °C ([TT-501](../03-instrumentation/xmeas-18-stripper-temperature.md))
- **Steam Flow Control:** [FV-502 (XMV-09)](../04-final-control-elements/xmv-09-stripper-steam-valve.md)
- **Product Bottoms Level Control:** [LV-501 (XMV-08)](../04-final-control-elements/xmv-08-stripper-underflow-valve.md) feeding Surge Drum [D-601](d-601-product-surge-drum.md).
- **Overpressure Relief:** [PSV-501](../06-safety-instrumented-system/psv-501.md) set at 2.20 MPag.
