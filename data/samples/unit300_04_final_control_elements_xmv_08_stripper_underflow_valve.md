# XMV-08 (LV-501) — Stripper Product Bottoms Control Valve

- **Tag:** LV-501 (DCS Output: XMV-08)
- **Description:** 4-inch Jacketed Control Valve regulating stripped resin product (Stream 11) from T-501 to D-601.
- **Fail Position:** Fail Closed (FC). Normal position: 50% Open. Controlled by level loop [LIC-501](../05-control-loops/loop-lic-501-stripper-level.md).
